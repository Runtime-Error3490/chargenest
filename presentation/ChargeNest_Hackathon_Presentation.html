AI-Powered Features

Three AI Agents Working For You

🤖

Charging Concierge

Natural language booking assistant that understands your needs

  • ✨ "Find a charger near Yeshwantpur under $20"
  • ✨ Ranked by power, distance, rating & price (in code, repeatable)
  • ✨ Book, cancel, top up, report issues via chat
  • ✨ Place name → real coordinates, uses your location
  • ✨ Remembers context, streams replies, asks before spending
⚖️

Dispute Resolver

Fair, fast conflict resolution with AI analysis

  • ✨ Analyzes booking, session & audit trail
  • ✨ 95%+ confidence = auto-resolve + refund
  • ✨ Transparent reasoning shown to users
  • ✨ Admin fallback for edge cases
  • ✨ Hosts send PayPal damage invoices instead
💡

Pricing Advisor

Helps hosts set competitive, fair prices

  • ✨ Analyzes local market rates
  • ✨ Factors in charger power tier
  • ✨ Location-aware suggestions
  • ✨ Plain-language explanations
  • ✨ Host always has final say

  • 🔐

    Human Approval — Booking & payments ask first; invoices always do
  • 📋
